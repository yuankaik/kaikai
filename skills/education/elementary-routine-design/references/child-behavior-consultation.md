# Child Behavior Consultation Pattern

When a parent reports concerning child behavior (language extremes, aggression talk, peer issues), use this workflow to provide evidence-based analysis grounded in platform consensus + developmental psychology.

## Workflow

### 1. Search multi-platform for the specific behavior

Search ALL three platforms:
```
mcp__cn_scraper__zhihu_search(keyword="小学生 说狠话 语言暴力 心理 10岁")
mcp__cn_scraper__xiaohongshu_search(keyword="孩子说脏话狠话 小学生 家长怎么办")
mcp__cn_scraper__weibo_search(keyword="小学生 口头禅 毒 烂梗")
```

Key search terms:
- Platform consensus tags: 烂梗对孩子的危害, 小学生口头禅, 央视点名
- Parent perspective: 孩子说脏话怎么办, 小学生顶嘴
- Expert sources: 李玫瑾, 海北老师, 北京儿童医院

### 2. Synthesize platform findings with developmental psychology

Map behavior to developmental stage:

| Age | Stage | Common language behaviors |
|-----|-------|--------------------------|
| 9-11 | Pre-adolescence | Testing language power, discovering words as weapons, peer contagion |
| 11-13 | Early adolescence | Identity testing, boundary pushing, online/gaming language |

**The "语言试枪" (Language as Weapon) Framework:**

For 10-year-olds (四升五), extreme language like "杀了你" or "灭你全家" is typically NOT genuine violence. It's "语言试枪" — the child has just discovered that words can produce power. Previously, "I hate you" got no reaction; now "I'll kill your whole family" makes people freeze. This power discovery is intoxicating and the child tests it repeatedly.

Key insight: **语言调控系统 and 情感系统 develop at different rates.** Emotional empathy (caring, sharing) is already developed. Language impulse control (prefrontal cortex) matures at ~25. This explains the "嘴上流里流气" + "内心善良" coexistence — it's developmental desync, not moral failure.

### 3. Source analysis (ranked by likelihood)

| Source | Likelihood | Notes |
|--------|:--:|-------|
| 同学/玩伴 (peers) | Highest | Age 10: peer influence exceeds parental influence |
| 短视频/游戏 (short videos/gaming) | Medium | Gaming voice chat, short video comments are cesspools |
| 试探家长底线 (testing boundaries) | Medium | Testing "what happens when I say this to dad?" |
| 内心真实暴力 (genuine violence) | Very low | Only if accompanied by actual physical aggression |

### 4. Correction strategy

**Cold processing (冷处理) — the core technique:**
When child says "I'll kill them," respond with calm, boring logic: "Oh, how would you do it? Knife or gun? What about the body? What about the police?" Don't get angry — turn the dramatic statement into a boring logic puzzle. After 2-3 times, the weapon loses its power.

**Give replacement phrases:**
| Scene | Don't say | Say instead |
|-------|-----------|-------------|
| Pool lane occupied | 赶走他们 | 我们先到，麻烦让一下 |
| Someone comments | 杀了你 | 你这样说我不舒服 |
| Very angry | 灭你全家 | 我现在特别生气，先别跟我说话 |

**Distinguish words from actions:**
The critical question: "Does he act on it?" If NO physical violence, no property destruction, no animal cruelty — this is a language phase, not a character problem. Tell the child: "Dad knows you don't mean it, but others won't know. Do you want others to think you're that kind of person?"

**Clean the environment:**
Check phone/tablet content. Gaming streamers and comedy short videos are primary sources of extreme language. Replace with better content, don't just ban.

### 5. Timeframe

| Phase | Timeline | Expectation |
|-------|----------|-------------|
| Frequency drop | 1-2 weeks | Child finds cold processing boring, uses phrases less |
| Near resolution | ~1 month | Occasional relapses with peers only |
| School restart | After summer | New environment, new teacher → natural convergence |

### 6. What NOT to do

| Wrong approach | Why it fails |
|----------------|--------------|
| 暴怒/打骂 (rage/punish) | Shows child the words HAVE power over adults |
| "你怎么变坏了" (you've become bad) | Labels create negative self-identity |
| Debate "杀人对不对" (is killing wrong?) | Child already knows — this is about testing, not ethics |
| Ban all such words | The forbidden fruit effect — more tempting |
