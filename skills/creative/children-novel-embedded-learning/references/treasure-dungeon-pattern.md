# Treasure Dungeon Pattern: Earned, Not Bought

When the user says a treasure origin is "不够惊天" or "太随便了", the fix is a dungeon — not a description.

## Trigger

- User says a key item's origin feels "商店买的" (store-bought), "不够燃" (not epic enough), or "糊弄不了孩子" (a kid won't buy it)
- A major artifact needs a backstory that matches its power level

## Pattern: The Forbidden Door

Applied to 永夜伞 in 深海钓徒 V3. The item was originally on a shelf in Octo's shop — the user rejected this as unworthy of an ultimate treasure.

### Structure

1. **Location**: Hidden behind ordinary-looking facade (market → secret stone door → tunnel → void)
2. **Gatekeeping**: Only the rod-bearer may enter. Previous entrants (7 guardians) failed and came back "empty"
3. **Previous failures on the walls**: Inscription trail of everyone who tried and failed. Creates weight and stakes.
4. **The oldest note is from the master**: The predecessor (grandfather) left a note on the wall — "往前。别停。" — confirming this is the right path but NOT solving it for the protagonist
5. **Trial, not combat**: The darkness doesn't fight — it ASKS. Uses the protagonist's deepest fears (teacher's criticism, mother's disappointed face, self-doubt). The answer is not "I win" but "I accept" (受着)
6. **Guardian release**: The previous failed entrants are released when the trial is passed — they nod and dissolve. Closure for them, weight for the moment.
7. **The treasure opens ITSELF**: Not taken — it chooses. First opening in 3000 years.
8. **Revelation of shared origin**: The umbrella and the rod are the same material, same maker, separated for 3000 years. The discovery of this connection is part of the trial's reward.
9. **Return with physical evidence**: The cave collapses behind the protagonist. The market witnesses the return. The shopkeeper who stored it for centuries is physically transformed (Octo's tentacles untie for first time).
10. **New status**: "Nobody buys the umbrella." — the item cannot be priced or sold. It was never merchandise.

## Key Rules

- **No purchasing.** Treasures at this tier cannot be acquired through commerce. Trade is for consumables (墨晶), not ultimate artifacts.
- **No passive receipt.** The protagonist must walk through the door. The treasure doesn't come to them.
- **Trial must be internal.** Physical combat is for BOSS fights. Dungeon trials for artifacts should test character: fear, doubt, identity. The answer should be the protagonist's core philosophy (受着), not a clever tactic.
- **The predecessor's shadow.** The master/grandfather figure should have been here first and left a marker, but couldn't finish. The protagonist goes further.
- **Witnesses matter.** The return moment must have observers who understand what just happened (Octo's reaction, 河童's notebook).
