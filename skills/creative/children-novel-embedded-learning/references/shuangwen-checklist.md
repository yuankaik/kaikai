# 爽文 Checklist: Talent + Grind + Surpass

When the user says a draft "还不是爽文" or "不够爽", check these three axes. If any is missing, the fix is structural, not tonal.

## The Triad

### 1. Innate Gift (天赋异禀)

The protagonist has something they were born with — not taught, not acquired.

**深海钓徒 example**: 佳乐 sees the character 生 in moss on a turtle shell at the water museum. No one else sees it. It's not the rod — it's his own perception. Later confirmed: 大公龟's shell inscriptions glow for him but never glowed for the 11 previous rod-bearers.

**Rule**: The gift must appear in Chapter 1-2, before any external power source activates. The reader should feel: "He was already special before the rod found him."

**Anti-pattern**: Rod chooses him, rod gives him power, rod explains everything. This makes the rod the hero, not the protagonist.

### 2. Visible Training / Grind (努力修炼)

Between acquiring a power and mastering it, the protagonist must fail publicly.

**深海钓徒 example**: 
- Chapter 4: First attempt at word-glyph synthesis — feeds "团" into pot. Pot EXPLODES, arm numb for 10 seconds. 河童: "Pot has taste."
- Chapter 6-8: Two more failed attempts. "团" → pot rejects and blows rust off a cannon. "蝠" → pot warms but doesn't synthesize. Pot is teaching him through failure.
- Chapter 9: Third attempt succeeds. "暗" + "破" → "灭". The two earlier explosions were preheating.

**Rule**: At minimum 1 failure before every major power unlock. The failure should be visible to companions (河童 records it).

**Anti-pattern**: Protagonist understands power immediately, uses it perfectly first try. This is "chosen one" passivity, not 爽文 agency.

### 3. Surpass the Predecessor (超越前人 / 巅峰存在)

By the end, the protagonist must go beyond what the master figure could do.

**深海钓徒 example**:
- Grandpa (rod's original owner) left notes throughout the deep sea: "等", "沧龙", "往前。别停。" — he scouted the path but couldn't finish.
- Chapter 15: 海神 pauses before responding to the 12th name. The 11 previous names were just names — 佳乐 is the first to write HIMSELF into the name. Even the old rod's owner never did this.
- Chapter 17: Grandpa's final note changes from "造化完整了" to "你已经走到我看不到的地方了。造化——不是我给你。是你自己写完的。"

**Rule**: The predecessor's shadow must be visible from early chapters (notes, coats, rod markings). The surpassing moment must be explicit — either the master says it, or the world responds in a way it never has before.

**Anti-pattern**: Protagonist follows master's path to completion. Master says "well done, you finished what I started." This is apprentice, not peak existence.

## Quick Diagnostic

When user says "还不够爽":
1. Does the protagonist have a gift visible BEFORE any external power activates? If no → add to Ch1-2.
2. Are there scenes of the protagonist FAILING to use their power, in front of witnesses? If no → insert training failure before next power unlock.
3. Does the predecessor/master figure explicitly say the protagonist has gone beyond them? If no → change the final message.

If all three are present and the user still says it's not爽文 → the fight scenes need rewriting (see yinian-yongheng-recipe.md).
