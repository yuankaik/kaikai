# 无厘头 3% 技法 (Absurdist Spice)

When the reader says the humor is good but needs "a little more randomness / 无厘头", add ~3% absurdist humor — tiny touches that don't affect plot but add texture.

## Core Rule

**3% of text, 0% of plot impact.** These are sprinkles on top, not new story elements. Each insertion is 1-3 lines max. Never during a boss fight climax or emotional peak.

## Technique Catalog

### 1. Sentient Notebook (河童's notebook writes back)
Once or twice: 河童 opens his notebook and there's a note he didn't write. Short, creepy, never explained.
- "你今天斗笠歪了。" He screams.
- "别再写我了。" He closes it slowly.

### 2. Brand Name Roulette (吴薯片's chips change brand)
Every time 吴薯片's chips are mentioned, use a different real brand name. Never acknowledge it.
- Ch1: "乐事的"
- Ch2: "可比克"  
- Ch5: "上好佳的"
河童 notices and writes: "Parallel universe convenience store theory."

### 3. Disclaimer Creep (朱八爪's sign gets longer)
The disclaimer on a fake product grows each time it appears, wrapping around the sign.
- First: "永夜伞复制品——百分之百防水"
- Later: "(注：深海水不算水，算环境。星光不算光，算装饰。手持不算持，算展示。)"

### 4. Almost Speaks (比目鱼 nearly talks)
比目鱼's mouth opens, a bubble comes out, silence.
佳乐: "你说啥？"
比目鱼: ...
佳乐: "算了当我没问。"

### 5. Official Measurement Chart (Sword's angle quantified)
河童 creates a measurement system for Sword's剑尖 angle.
- "偏二度=微笑。偏五度=大笑。偏十度=你这辈子值了。偏十五度=剑鱼在考虑转行。"

### 6. Wrong Output (锅 produces something unexpected)
锅 explodes and instead of a word, produces a small confused-looking fish. The fish looks at everyone, looks at锅, swims away. Never mentioned again.

### 7. Mood Flower (大公龟's flower changes color)
河童 keeps a color chart. 佳乐 never notices.
- "淡黄=平静。橘色=好奇。暗红=要撞人了。"

### 8. Wrong Topic (竿子 almost says something mundane)
The ancient rod starts to say "今天天气——" instead of "造化——" and cuts off.
佳乐: "你刚才是不是想说天气？"
竿子: (silence)

### 9. Warranty Label (爷爷's茶壶 has a label)
"宜家。三千年质保。还剩两天。"
Never explained why an ancient artifact has IKEA branding.

### 10. Inexplicable Talent (minor character has random skill)
- 河童 can whistle underwater. Tries to explain the physics. Gives up.
- 吴薯片 can count薯片 by smell. Accurate to ±1 chip.

## Application Rules

- **Density**: 8-12 insertions across a ~40K word book (~3%)
- **Timing**: During transitions, quiet moments, or victory aftermaths
- **NEVER**: During boss fight climax, emotional peak (海神 scene), or Ch17 classroom payoff
- **Format**: 1-3 lines each. No setup, no payoff, no callback needed
- **Tone**: Deadpan delivery. The absurdity comes from the straight-faced presentation

## Why It Works

无厘头 serves as palate cleanser between high-intensity comedy beats. When every line tries to be funny, the big moments get diluted. Small, inexplicable touches create breathing room while maintaining the "anything can happen" atmosphere of 爽文.

## Session References

- 传闻→剧情影响: Rumors must change behavior, not just narrate. BOSSes check mouths, merchants pre-stock, fish retreat from numbers. Each rumor needs one concrete world consequence.
- 书名=口头禅: When catchphrase IS the book soul, use it as title. "受着呗" > "深海钓徒". Subtitle = opening image ("一支漏水的水笔").
- 爷爷批改战术笔记: Ultimate comic reveal — ancestor's 3000-year notebook gets反向更新 by grandson's歪招. Proves超越 without preaching.
